# Security

Found a security problem in anything Nectarmark Studio builds or publishes? Please email
[hello@nectarmark.com](mailto:hello@nectarmark.com) with "Security" in the subject, and don't open a public issue.

Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it affects. We'll reply within two business days and keep you
updated until it's fixed. Please give us a reasonable chance to fix it before you share it publicly.
