Most web designers send a proposal. We send a website.

We pick a local business and learn it the way a customer would: the hours, the menu, the services, the street it's
on. Then we build the site before we ever knock on the door, and show the owner. If they love it, it goes live on
their own domain within days. If they don't, it comes down. No meetings, no mockups, no six-week wait.

## What every site comes with

- **A design that's only yours.** Custom illustration, colors and type picked for your business. No two of our
  sites share a font or a lead color, and we test for that.
- **Built for the phone call.** Tap-to-call, directions and today's hours sit where customers look first, on any
  screen.
- **Edit it from your phone.** Change your hours, menu, prices or services yourself. You sign in with a link in your
  email, so there's no password to lose.
- **Fast, accessible and easy to find.** Every site is checked against WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility and loads fast.
  Each one also tells search engines exactly what the business is, where it is and when it's open.
- **Yours to keep.** Your domain is registered in your name. If you ever want to take the whole site and go, one
  buy-out and we hand over the code and the content.
- **A real person nearby.** When something needs changing, you ask someone down the road, not a support queue.

## For developers

One [Payload](https://payloadcms.com) CMS runs every client site. Each site is its own Next.js app on Vercel, and
an edit in the CMS shows up on the next page view. We're getting our passwordless sign-in for Payload,
`payload-plugin-magic-link`, ready to release as open source. Watch this org if that would help you.
